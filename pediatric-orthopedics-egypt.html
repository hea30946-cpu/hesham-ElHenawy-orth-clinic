Pediatric Orthopedic Care in Alexandria, Egypt

Dr. Hesham El Henawy provides pediatric orthopedic and limb deformity assessment in Alexandria, Egypt. International families may contact the clinic to ask about arranging an in-person appointment.

Contact via WhatsApp

Areas of assessment

Bow legs in children

Age-appropriate assessment of lower-limb alignment and growth.

Intoeing gait

Evaluation of foot, tibial and femoral rotational patterns.

Congenital and limb deformities

Individual assessment and discussion of possible next steps.

Visiting from abroad

Contact the clinic to confirm availability and location before booking travel. Assessment and any treatment recommendations depend on an in-person clinical examination where indicated.
